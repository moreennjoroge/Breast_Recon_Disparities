# Reporting Guidelines and Checklists for Plastic Surgery Research

## Overview

High-quality plastic surgery research follows established reporting guidelines from the EQUATOR Network (Enhancing the QUAlity and Transparency Of health Research). These guidelines ensure transparent, complete, and reproducible reporting.

## When to Use Each Guideline

### CONSORT (Consolidated Standards of Reporting Trials)
**Use for:** Randomized controlled trials

**Key elements:**
- Title: Include "randomized" in title
- Abstract: Structured with specific RCT elements
- Trial design (parallel, factorial, crossover, etc.)
- Participants (eligibility, recruitment, settings)
- Interventions (detailed description of each arm)
- Outcomes (primary and secondary, pre-specified)
- Sample size calculation and justification
- Randomization method and allocation concealment
- Blinding procedures
- Statistical methods
- Participant flow (CONSORT diagram required)
- Baseline characteristics table
- Outcomes and estimation with CI
- Harms/adverse events
- Trial registration number

**CONSORT Checklist:** 25 items
**Website:** http://www.consort-statement.org/

### STROBE (Strengthening the Reporting of Observational Studies in Epidemiology)
**Use for:** 
- Cohort studies
- Case-control studies  
- Cross-sectional studies

**Key elements:**
- Title and abstract: Study design in title
- Background/rationale
- Objectives
- Study design described early in methods
- Setting and dates
- Eligibility criteria
- Variables defined (outcomes, exposures, confounders)
- Data sources and measurement
- Bias: How addressed
- Study size justification
- Statistical methods for confounding
- Participant flow diagram
- Characteristics of participants
- Outcome data
- Main results with CI and p-values
- Other analyses (subgroup, interactions)
- Key results summary
- Limitations
- Interpretation and generalizability

**STROBE Checklist:** 22 items (varies by design)
**Website:** https://www.strobe-statement.org/

### CARE (Case Report)
**Use for:** Case reports and case series

**Key elements:**
- Title: Include "case report" or "case series"
- Keywords
- Abstract: Structured (Introduction, Patient, Discussion, Conclusion)
- Patient information (demographics, presenting complaints)
- Clinical findings (physical examination, imaging)
- Timeline (key dates and interventions)
- Diagnostic assessment
- Therapeutic interventions
- Follow-up and outcomes
- Patient perspective (when possible)
- Informed consent obtained

**CARE Checklist:** 13 items
**Website:** https://www.care-statement.org/

### PRISMA (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses)
**Use for:**
- Systematic reviews
- Meta-analyses

**Key elements:**
- Protocol registration (PROSPERO)
- Title: Include "systematic review" or "meta-analysis"
- Structured abstract
- Introduction: Rationale and objectives
- Methods:
  - Eligibility criteria
  - Information sources
  - Search strategy (full search for at least one database)
  - Study selection process
  - Data collection process
  - Data items sought
  - Risk of bias assessment
  - Summary measures
  - Synthesis methods
  - Publication bias assessment
- Results:
  - Study selection (PRISMA flow diagram required)
  - Study characteristics
  - Risk of bias in studies
  - Results of syntheses
  - Additional analyses
- Discussion: Limitations, conclusions
- Funding

**PRISMA Checklist:** 27 items
**Website:** http://www.prisma-statement.org/

### ARRIVE (Animal Research: Reporting of In Vivo Experiments)
**Use for:** Animal studies

**Key elements:**
- Title: Include species
- Abstract: Full summary
- Ethical statement
- Study design (objectives, hypothesis)
- Experimental procedures (detailed)
- Experimental animals (species, strain, source)
- Housing and husbandry
- Sample size calculation
- Allocating animals to groups
- Experimental outcomes (defined)
- Statistical methods
- Baseline data
- Numbers analyzed
- Outcomes and estimation
- Adverse events

**ARRIVE Checklist:** 20 items
**Website:** https://arriveguidelines.org/

### STROCSS (Strengthening the Reporting of Cohort Studies in Surgery)
**Use for:** Surgical cohort studies (augments STROBE)

**Surgery-specific additions:**
- Surgical technique details
- Surgeon experience/volume
- Learning curve considerations
- Device/implant specifications
- Perioperative care protocols
- Technical success definition
- Surgeon-reported outcomes

**STROCSS Checklist:** 17 items
**Website:** https://www.strocssguideline.com/

## Study-Specific Considerations for Plastic Surgery

### Breast Reconstruction Studies
**Additional elements:**
- Reconstruction timing (immediate vs delayed)
- Type of reconstruction (implant vs autologous)
- Radiation history
- Bilateral vs unilateral
- Patient-reported outcomes (BREAST-Q)
- Complications classified by Clavien-Dindo
- Definition of surgical success/failure
- Revision rates and reasons

### Aesthetic Surgery Studies
**Additional elements:**
- Patient selection criteria
- Preoperative assessment methods
- Standardized photography protocol
- Objective outcome measures (when possible)
- Patient-reported outcomes (validated scales)
- Complication classification
- Definition of satisfactory result
- Revision rates

### Craniofacial Studies
**Additional elements:**
- Age at intervention
- Staging of procedures
- Multidisciplinary team involvement
- Growth considerations
- Functional outcomes (speech, breathing, feeding)
- Psychosocial outcomes
- Long-term follow-up results

### Hand Surgery Studies
**Additional elements:**
- Injury mechanism and classification
- Time to intervention
- Functional assessment methods (grip strength, ROM)
- Return to work/function
- Cold intolerance
- Patient-reported outcomes (QuickDASH, MHQ)

## Common Outcome Measures in Plastic Surgery

### Patient-Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs)

**General:**
- SF-36 (Short Form 36)
- SF-12 (Short Form 12)
- EQ-5D (EuroQol-5 Dimension)

**Breast:**
- BREAST-Q (reconstruction, augmentation, reduction, conservation)
- Satisfaction with Breast Questionnaire

**Body Contouring:**
- BODY-Q
- Appearance Evaluation subscale (MBSRQ-AS)

**Face:**
- FACE-Q
- Facial Clinimetric Evaluation Scale (FACES)
- Derriford Appearance Scale

**Hand:**
- Michigan Hand Outcomes Questionnaire (MHQ)
- QuickDASH (Disabilities of the Arm, Shoulder, and Hand)
- Patient-Rated Wrist Evaluation (PRWE)

**Burns:**
- Burn Specific Health Scale-Brief (BSHS-B)
- Satisfaction with Appearance Scale (SWAP)

**Scars:**
- Patient and Observer Scar Assessment Scale (POSAS)
- Vancouver Scar Scale (VSS)

### Objective Measures

**Photography:**
- Standardized lighting and positioning
- Consistent camera settings
- Multiple views documented
- Anthropometric measurements when applicable

**Functional Assessments:**
- Range of motion (goniometry)
- Strength (dynamometry)
- Two-point discrimination
- Sensation testing (Semmes-Weinstein monofilaments)

**Quality of Life:**
- Physical function scales
- Psychological well-being
- Social function
- Return to work/activities

## Complication Reporting

### Clavien-Dindo Classification
Use for standardized complication severity reporting:

- **Grade I**: Any deviation from normal postoperative course without intervention
- **Grade II**: Requiring pharmacological treatment
- **Grade III**: Requiring surgical, endoscopic, or radiological intervention
  - IIIa: Without general anesthesia
  - IIIb: Under general anesthesia
- **Grade IV**: Life-threatening complication requiring ICU
  - IVa: Single organ dysfunction
  - IVb: Multi-organ dysfunction
- **Grade V**: Death

### Plastic Surgery-Specific Complications

**Microvascular Surgery:**
- Total vs partial flap loss
- Vascular compromise (timing, intervention)
- Secondary procedures required

**Implant-Based Reconstruction:**
- Infection (with/without explantation)
- Capsular contracture (Baker grade)
- Implant malposition
- Rippling/palpability
- Expander/implant removal

**Autologous Reconstruction:**
- Fat necrosis
- Hernia/bulge at donor site
- Donor site morbidity
- Flap volume loss

## Statistical Reporting Requirements

### Descriptive Statistics
- Mean ± standard deviation for normal distributions
- Median (interquartile range) for skewed distributions
- Proportions as n/N (percentage)
- 95% confidence intervals for estimates

### Comparative Statistics
**Continuous variables:**
- T-test (parametric, two groups)
- ANOVA (parametric, >2 groups)
- Mann-Whitney U (non-parametric, two groups)
- Kruskal-Wallis (non-parametric, >2 groups)

**Categorical variables:**
- Chi-square test
- Fisher's exact test (small samples)

**Survival/Time-to-event:**
- Kaplan-Meier curves
- Log-rank test
- Cox proportional hazards

**Correlation:**
- Pearson (parametric)
- Spearman (non-parametric)

### Multivariable Analysis
- Multiple linear regression (continuous outcomes)
- Logistic regression (binary outcomes)
- Report adjusted odds ratios or hazard ratios with 95% CI
- Include all variables in model
- Address multicollinearity

### Sample Size and Power
- A priori calculation preferred
- State: α level, power (1-β), effect size, final n
- Post-hoc power calculations discouraged

## Data Presentation Standards

### Tables
- Table 1: Patient demographics and baseline characteristics
- Clearly label groups being compared
- Include p-values for between-group comparisons
- Note statistical test used in footnote
- Define all abbreviations

### Figures
- High-quality, professional images
- Before/after photos with standardized positioning
- Graphs with clear axes labels
- Error bars defined (SD vs SEM vs CI)
- Statistical significance markers explained

## Ethical Reporting

### Human Subjects
- IRB/Ethics committee approval number
- Study conducted per Declaration of Helsinki
- Informed consent obtained from all participants
- Clinical trial registration number (if applicable)
- Data safety monitoring (for trials)

### Patient Privacy
- No identifiable information in text
- Patient consent for photos
- Face obscured if identity protection needed
- Case details modified to protect identity

### Conflicts of Interest
- Financial relationships disclosed
- Industry funding acknowledged
- Consultant relationships noted
- Stock ownership/equity disclosed

### Author Contributions
Use CRediT taxonomy:
- Conceptualization
- Methodology
- Investigation
- Data curation
- Formal analysis
- Writing – original draft
- Writing – review & editing
- Supervision
- Funding acquisition

## Checklist for Manuscript Submission

Before submitting, ensure:
- [ ] Appropriate reporting guideline identified
- [ ] Corresponding checklist completed
- [ ] Checklist included as supplemental material
- [ ] Location of each checklist item noted in manuscript
- [ ] All required elements from guideline included
- [ ] Statistical methods appropriate and described
- [ ] Ethical approvals documented
- [ ] Patient consents obtained
- [ ] Conflicts of interest disclosed
- [ ] Author contributions specified
